# Ace Pro Support

Ace Pro imports a protected local copy of a tennis video and runs Apple Vision analysis directly on the iPhone. Videos are not uploaded by the release application.

## Getting started

1. Open **Today** or **Library** and choose **Add a tennis video**.
2. Select a video from Photos or Files and save the session.
3. Open the session and choose **On-device video analysis**.
4. Select a 15, 30, or 60 second segment and run analysis.
5. Open a motion range to play that short interval, or open a pose result to pause on its measured frame.

Stable landscape footage with the full court visible generally produces the clearest measurements.

## What the results mean

- Motion candidates are generic parabolic trajectories. They are not confirmed tennis balls.
- Body and hand poses are visible landmark measurements. They do not diagnose technique.
- Scene-motion values can reflect the player, the ball, spectators, or camera movement.
- Apple Intelligence descriptions are optional, generated on the device, and only available on eligible devices with a supported iOS version.

## Troubleshooting

- If no motion candidate appears, try a brighter, steadier clip where the ball is visible for consecutive frames.
- If no pose appears, use footage where the player is larger and unobstructed.
- Keep Ace Pro open while analysis is running.
- If a local video is missing, import the original again. Ace Pro does not synchronize or recover deleted videos.
